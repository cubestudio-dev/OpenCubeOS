Notes:
  - ConOut and the plain COM1 serial render BOTH appear in the QEMU
    -serial capture (OVMF mirrors ConOut to the serial line) - the
    menu is visually identical to the BIOS flavor's VGA output.
  - The countdown runs on the boot-services timer event (100 ms
    grain, polled); timer-cancel + armed semantics unchanged.
  - Shutdown is the REAL ACPI S5 under UEFI too: the RSDP comes from
    the EFI configuration table (efi/ocbl_rsdp_efi.c) and the
    FADT/_S5/PM1a walk is the shared common/ocbl_acpi.c.
  - The i6300esb watchdog probe+arm (60 s) runs before the menu;
    QEMU has no 6300ESB so it reports absent (honest no-op).
  - menu_drive.py gained the OCBL_OVMF=1 flavor (same driver, PS/2
    sendkey path through OVMF's ConIn) - live-caught on the way:
    the toolchain -L data dirs are required even with -bios OVMF
    (missing vgabios-stdvga.bin killed QEMU before the socket).
