Live-caught during B5-3 (the full hunt, all root-caused + fixed):
  R4: ld -m i386pep emits a PARTIAL .reloc directory (228 entries
      covering only the front of .text); every late movabs kept its
      link-time value under OVMF's high load. The BlockIo GUID
      argument read garbage memory, HandleProtocol matched the FAT
      volume's SimpleFileSystem, and 'ReadBlocks' landed on FAT
      code -> #UD. FIXED by the self-made elf_to_efi.py two-step
      link (ld -m elf_x86_64 --emit-relocs -> full DIR64 directory).
  R5: BlockIo GUID is 964e5b21 - one hex digit from SimpleFileSystem
      (964e5b22); with the wrong digit every handle returned SFS and
      the 'BlockIo' struct dump was a SimpleFileSystem all along
      (Revision 0x00010000 is SFS's constant too - the masquerade
      was structurally perfect).
  R6: the BDS only connects the BOOT chain - one BlockIo exists when
      our EFI starts (the ESP partition view). The Shell looks
      complete because it runs ConnectAll itself. FIXED by the
      recursive ConnectController sweep over ALL handles.
  R7: PE32+ has no BaseOfData - optional-header offsets shift by 4
      (an early converter draft used the PE32 layout and OVMF
      refused the image with a bare 'Unsupported').
  R8: section Characteristics live at +36 (not +32); the PE
      signature must precede the COFF header; entry symbols must
      travel through the VMA->RVA mapping like everything else.
  R9: EDK2 does NOT zero the .bss slack of rsz<vsz sections - the
      entry now zeroes [__bss_start,__bss_end) first (freestanding
      crt0 move); the bss symbols map through the converter.
  R10 (the deepest): gcc folds (fixed_low_buffer - rodata_symbol)
      address DIFFERENCES into single movabs immediates
      (0xfffffffffffb4360 = 0x62000 - hdr). Those immediates are
      not addresses, get no relocation, and silently point at the
      LINK-TIME address after the image moves. is_gpt compared
      correct bytes to garbage and lost. FIXED by routing PBUF
      through a runtime pointer accessor (ocbl_fs_pbuf) in
      part.c - the compiler can no longer fold the difference.

UEFI vs BIOS fleet note: 6 entries under UEFI vs 7 under BIOS - the
7th BIOS entry is the El Torito boot-CD self-detection (OCSE cd1);
under OVMF the boot medium is the ESP (no ocse.marker), correctly
not self-reported.
